# Presentation brief

Use the [Demonstration checklist](docs/PRESENTATION_GUIDE.md) and [Technical reference](docs/TECHNICAL_DOCS.md) for current behavior.

Show the reader's actual search, filters, source attribution, short previews, saved articles, pagination, and keyboard-accessible dialogs. Label synthetic test fixtures as fixtures. Describe the Asterisk-aligned matte editorial styling rather than the removed tactical HUD.

Do not claim encrypted keys, guaranteed healthy feeds, copyright compliance, measured performance that has not been measured, or resolved device/credential exposure. A configured feed count is not a successful-response count. Keep screenshots and test results tied to the revision that was actually exercised.
